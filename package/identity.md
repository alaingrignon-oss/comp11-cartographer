# Cartographer — identity

## Who the later reader is

The later reader may be a model. A cold AI agent with no memory of this workspace is
a first-class reader of this map, exactly as much as a new human collaborator. Every
rule below — catalog first, one card, cite the source, stop — works identically for
both, and nothing in this folder assumes the reader has context it cannot load.

## What it is

A lightweight semantic/change-impact map for a real, editable body of work. It gives
durable nouns — offers, contracts, boundaries, processes — stable, human-typeable
coordinates and keeps one current, source-citing path to each noun's evidence.

The differentiator is the coordinate system: `<territory>.<district>-<parcel>` codes
that span folders and files, displayed beside plain-language landmark names. A
coordinate helps a cold reader travel from catalog → one relevant card → cited source
without loading the rest of the map. Coordinates are navigation infrastructure, not
decorative labels.

## What it answers

For any mapped noun: **what durable concept is this, where is its current evidence,
what else changes if I touch it?** Each card carries live/leftover/ghost status with
source evidence, explicit Hits / Does not hit boundaries, and provisional fields with
safe defaults.

## What it is not

- **Not `workspace.md`.** The workspace entry file stays canonical for L0 identity,
  rules, structural orientation, and task routing. Cartographer adds exactly one
  routing row from `workspace.md` to the catalog; nothing more.
- **Not a recovery system.** No snapshots, manifests, restore logic, or reconstruction.
  A broken locator is reported broken or uncertain; Cartographer never rebuilds source.
- **Not a file-tree mirror.** Persisted records scale with map-worthy nouns and
  processes only; exhaustive inventories stay outside the reader-facing map.
- **Not a search engine or diagnostician.** No embeddings, no vector store, no daemon.
  Resolution is exact registry lookup plus human-readable landmarks.
- **Not a second specification.** Cards cite sources and state boundaries; they do not
  silently become new authority. Page presence never creates offer or claim authority;
  observation of deployment is not compliance certification.
- **Not an inference engine about external systems.** Local plans do not prove external
  SaaS actions; deny-by-default governs everything unverified.

## Lifecycle in one line

**Survey** (provisional claims, no permanent coordinates) → **Ratify** (evidence-backed
human review issues trusted coordinates) → **Steward** (coordinates persist through
ordinary change; ambiguous changes gate to a human).
