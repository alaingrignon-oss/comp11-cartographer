# ULT.OFR-002 — Proof and claim boundary

**One sentence:** Proof and claim boundary is the durable rule that permits only current
narrow wording and policy language, confines internal results to attributed evidence,
defers offer contents to Card 1, and blocks unsubstantiated, unauthenticated, or
undeployed claims.

- **as_of:** 2026-08-20 · **status:** issued; active
- **Evidence grade:** A for the core boundary; C for internal/stale/draft claims; D for external/deployed fields
- **Aliases:** claim guardrails; proof policy; substantiation boundary; guarantee boundary; copy rules; testimonial boundary; shipping wording boundary

## Shape (authority-ordered)

1. `ULT.OFR-001` owns offer membership and current contents.
2. The dated product-truth copy contract owns allowed and prohibited product wording:
   allowed direction is qualitative only — "straighter and more consistent". Prohibited:
   distance/yardage promises, longer-drive promises, slice-stopping, immediate/first-use
   certainty, "lasts a lifetime" for spikes, technical manufacturing copy (3D printing /
   TPU / PLA), and claiming the refill QR before its print/scan test passes.
3. Final refund and shipping artifacts own current local non-financial policy wording:
   ninety-day satisfaction-based money-back guarantee; ships within one business day;
   five-to-seven-business-day standard delivery; approved refunds processed within five
   business days. These are wording authorities, not proof of publication.
4. Founder, field-test (eight golfers), instructor, and family assertions are
   attributed-only internal evidence — not independent substantiation.
5. Theme observations, Q&A artifacts, final-but-conflicted artifacts (e.g., a stale
   ten-spikes bundle quantity), drafts, and older specs reveal conflicts but cannot
   override higher authority.
6. Publication, deployment, template assignment, external reviews, named-quote
   provenance, refill-QR test status, and the free-shipping threshold remain unverified
   or withheld. Deny-by-default: say nothing stronger than the evidence.

## Hits

Product-page promises/benefits, proof blocks, Q&A/FAQ, guarantee/returns and shipping
wording, testimonials/review presentation, objection busters, packaging/features, and any
customer-facing offer description repeating a changed claim.

## Does not hit

Offer membership or contents as facts; monetary values or the shipping threshold value;
theme layout/code mechanics; live deployment or SaaS configuration; CAD/product design;
campaign execution; credentials; independent validation itself.
